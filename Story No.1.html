MYLA
SAMPLE STORY ALSO MY FIRST STORY
        ENJOY IT!
    
Part-1  The Luke’s rule
One day Luke goes to the market to buy some vegetables and snacks, 
but he only has money to buy half of the things, so he buys all things in his budget.

The next day

He eats some snacks in the morning and makes a dish in the evening. 
While he is cooking, his room’s doorbell rings, “ding-dong…” so he opens the door. A new roommate is there. His name was Roommate.

Roomate: Can I please stay here, please?
Luke: Ammm… Yes, but there are some rules.
Roomate: Which rules… ah, it’s OK, I will manage.
Luke: Rule no. 1 - Before 12:00 PM, sleep.
Rule no.2 - Don’t talk to another person in the room except me.
         Rule no. 3 - If you cook, so make the food for three persons: one for me, 
         one for you, and onefor…..
Roomate: Amm, OK.

One night
Roomate is cooking food at 12:30 PM because he forgot the rules. Luke is sleeping
Roomate: But wait, who are you?
Myla: Hi, I am Myla.
Roomate: Ahh, wait, what do you want? Some food or money or something else?
Myla: Nothing, bye.
Roomate: (cooking)
Myla: (Disappeared)

Part-2 What happened to Luke
Roomate: Good morning Luke
Luke: Good morning, bro. 😎
Roomate: You have a roommate name, Myla?
Luke: What!! What are you saying?? I only have one roommate, and it is you!!
Roomate: What? So who is Myla? I met her yesterday night. She only told me her 
name, and I don’t know where she disappeared.
Luke: (Scared) What did you say? M-M-Myla? What is the time when she came?
Roomate: Umm, I think 12:30 - 1:30 PM.
Luke: What did you do at that time?
Roommate: I cooked food for you. 👀
Luke: (Walks away)
Roommate: What happened to him?
[Roommate remembered the rules]
(Roommate is his room (thinking))

Next day…

Roommate: Good morning, Luke.

Luke: ……..
Roomate: Luke? Where are you?

Luke: (Missing)

Roomate: (Shouting) Luke, Luke, Luke…. Where are you?
Meanwhile Luke
Luke: Good morning, bro. 😎

Bro…. Where am I?

Is it a forest? (Walking)

[After coming out of forest]

Luke: What, who got me there… it’s a restricted forest. Many years ago,
 many forest keepers went inside and never came out. From that day, the forest is closed. 
 Everyone thinks there are many wild animals, but there are no animals.

Luke: Why am I here…

Meanwhile, Roommate

Roommate: (Calling police 911)

Police:Hello, what’s your emergency?

Roommate: M-M-My roommate is missing.

Police: OK, be calm. We are heading to your place.

12 min later

Police arrived.
Roommate: Tell everything.
Searching started. No evidence of Luke. The roommate took his complaint back.

Luke

(Scared)  searching for a taxi to go home. Finally!! One taxi arrived. He came home.

Part-3 → Who is Myla?

Roommate: (Crying) A-a-a, are you OK? (Crying)
Luke: Don’t cry. I am OK.
Roommate: It’s all my fault. If I don’t come, so nothing will happen.
Luke: Don’t say this. This is a long story about Myla. I can tell you after 3 hours.

3 hours later
(In the bedroom)

Luke: This is the story of Myla and . One day Ranjeet moved to India without telling Myla.
 He only left a note.

Note: Hi, Myla. I am moving to India. My flight is at 12:00 PM.

And the time when she is reading is 11:00. 
So she can take a taxi to the airport to say her last “goodbye”, but she was late by 32 min. 
So she became very sad and broke down completely. After that, something strange happened in the room.
 The lights started flickering, the room became extremely cold, and Myla suddenly disappeared.

Luke stopped speaking.

Roommate — What happened to Myla after that?

Luke — Nobody knows. But from that day, she started appearing to people who stayed in this room after midnight.
